01 / THE PROBLEM

Scams sound
like people.

Modern voice scams don't always look suspicious. They sound like a bank employee, a delivery agent, a government official - and they create urgency before the victim has time to think.

01

Trust is exploited

Attackers imitate familiar institutions and use convincing conversations to make fraudulent requests feel legitimate.

02

Pressure wins

Urgency, fear and social engineering push people toward decisions they would normally question.

03

Detection comes late

Most protection happens after a payment, message or report. Snitch AI moves detection into the conversation itself.

02 / HOW IT WORKS

From voice
to warning.

Snitch AI turns a live conversation into an interpretable risk signal - combining voice characteristics and suspicious conversational patterns into one clear score.

01

Capture

Analyze the incoming call stream without interrupting the conversation.

02

Understand

Extract spectral, prosodic and speaker-level signals from the voice.

03

Score

Combine detected signals into a simple 0–100 fraud-risk score.

04

Protect

Surface an alert when the conversation crosses a suspicious-risk threshold.
